01 / About

Behind the work.

Visual Research / 001
I make visual work that lives somewhere between design and feeling.

WEYA.exe is an independent art practice based in Thailand, focused on illustration, tattoo art, pattern design and visual experimentation.

My work is driven by line, texture, composition and the small imperfections that make an image feel human.

Each project starts from an idea and develops through sketching, visual research, composition and careful refinement.

— WEYA
